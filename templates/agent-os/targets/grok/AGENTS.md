## The estate — your own grown files

Copies of your own rules and what your source seat has learned live
beside this file, under `agent-os/estate/`: `CLAUDE-ESTATE.md` (your own
rules document), `LESSONS.md` and `LEARNINGS.md` (the grown flywheel),
and the `scripts/`, `lessons/` and `skills/` folders. The sync refreshes
them from your source seat every time it runs. Read
`agent-os/estate/CLAUDE-ESTATE.md` as your own standing rules, and reach
into the folders when a task touches them.
