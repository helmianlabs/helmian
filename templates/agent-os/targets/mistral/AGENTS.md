## The estate — your own grown files

Your own rules document and the flywheel companions ride the same sync
and land beside this file, under `agent-os/estate/`: `CLAUDE-ESTATE.md`
(your own rules document), `LESSONS.md` and `LEARNINGS.md` (the grown
flywheel), and the `scripts/`, `lessons/` and `skills/` folders. The
sync refreshes them from your source seat every time it runs. Read
`agent-os/estate/CLAUDE-ESTATE.md` as your own standing rules, and reach
into the folders when a task touches them.
