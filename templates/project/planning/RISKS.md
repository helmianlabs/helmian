## Open risks

Format:

```text
## Short title

**Risk:** what could go wrong.
**First signal:** what you would observe before it becomes expensive. A risk
with no observable signal is a hope, not a risk.
**If it fires:** the concrete response, including the rollback.
**Owner:** who watches for it.
```
