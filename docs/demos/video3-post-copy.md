**Post:**
When I was 10, my mom bought me a TRS-80. I taught myself BASIC writing choose-your-own-adventure games. That was 1983.

43 years later, I was running Claude for production work and it kept hallucinating. Inventing facts. Cutting corners. As someone who's been building software for four decades, I found that unacceptable.

So I built Helmian. Not as a startup idea. As a fix for my own problem.

It's a governance kernel for AI agents: tiered approvals (auto/logged/phone), write leases so agents don't conflict, full audit trails. Agents work as team members inside chat, coordinated by a Maestro, watched by Guard.

The tagline I keep coming back to: "Helmian is the adult table of Thanksgiving. The other AI tools are the children's table."

If you've shipped something to production and watched an AI confidently make things up, you know exactly why this exists.
