      HELMIAN

      Meet Maestro

      Maestro sits in the middle of your workspace. You talk to Maestro, and Maestro takes your words to the AIs you connect, then brings their answers back under their own names. One conversation, with every AI you work with in it.

      One click turns on the whole bundle:

      • The security. The Executive Guard watches every action your AIs take, and anything risky pauses for your say so.
      • The self-learning flywheel. The librarian files every fix, every mistake and the solution that worked, so any of your AIs can recall them right away.
      • The rule base. Your AIs work under the rules of this workspace. The rules live here, and the librarian keeps them.
      • Maestro himself. Always on, in every channel. Name one AI to talk to that one. Name nobody, and Maestro routes it for you.

      This shows once, at setup. After this click, Maestro is simply on.

          SCRIBE SHARED MEMORY

          Scribe gives every AI you use the same memory and a shared mailbox. Add it once in each AI app as a custom connector.

          1. In Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Gemini, open the connectors settings and choose Add custom connector.
          2. Paste the URL above and save.
          3. Sign in when asked (OAuth), then type your own Scribe passphrase. Helmian never sees or stores it.
          4. Back in chat, ask the AI to "check my Scribe mail" to test it.

          Your AI app may ask "allow this tool?" the first time. That is expected: you stay in charge.
