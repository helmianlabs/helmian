How charging works

The rules behind the numbers.

  1. 01

    Bring your own AI

    Helmian charges for governance, memory, connections, and Hands — not for tokens. Your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Mistral subscriptions, API keys, or local models stay yours, on your accounts.

  2. 02

    Approvals never switch off

    Governed actions are an included allowance, not the product. If an allowance runs out, approvals and the kill switch keep working — safety is never the thing that gets metered. Additional blocks of 10,000 actions are $49.

  3. 03

    Blocked actions are never billed

    An action Guard stops does not count against the allowance. You are never charged for being protected.

  4. 04

    Trials end in a downgrade, not a deletion

    When a trial or pilot ends, the workspace steps down to Taste. Rooms and history stay readable; the seats stop acting until a plan starts. Your memory is never held hostage.

  5. 05

    Prices rise for new cohorts, never on the people already in

    Founding customers keep the price they joined at. When a list price changes, it changes for the next cohort — the people who took the early risk keep their number.
