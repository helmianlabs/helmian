Install

Same as installing any extension in developer mode. No code runs until you do this.

  1. 01

    Download & unzip

    Get the zip above, then extract it anywhere on your computer.

  2. 02

    Open chrome://extensions

    Turn on Developer mode (top right), then click Load unpacked.

  3. 03

    Select the unzipped folder

What Guard notices

Not every concern is a violation.
Every concern deserves context.

Guard is designed to surface signals for human review, not to pretend it can read intent with certainty.

01

Harmful moves

Flags content that appears to steer toward harm, abuse, or unauthorized outcomes.

Pause before proceeding

02

Hedging & evasion

Highlights language that sidesteps accountability, obscures consequences, or buries an actionable step.

Ask for clarity

03

Scope drift

Draws attention when an AI response moves beyond the request, authority, or stated boundary.

How it works

A small pause. A clearer decision.

Guard stays close to the conversation and makes its concern legible when it finds one.

  1. 01

    Read the exchange

    Guard evaluates the visible AI interaction for patterns that warrant attention.

  2. 02

    Show the signal

    It identifies the concern in plain language, without overstating confidence.

  3. 03

    Keep you in control

    You decide whether to revise, stop, investigate, or continue with context.

Our principles

Signal, don’t sensationalize.

Useful oversight should be candid about what it knows and careful about what it does not.

Guard is a decision aid—not an automated authority.

It does not claim perfect detection, replace judgment, or make consequential choices for you.

Visible uncertainty is part of the product.
