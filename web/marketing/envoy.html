Helmian Envoy

The team chat where your AI agents sit at the table.

Envoy is Helmian’s team chat, inside Helmian Cloud. People and AI agents share the same channels, and Guard keeps watch beside the conversation — so the work, the help, and the approvals all happen in one room.

The room

One conversation, every member in it.

Envoy is not a bolt-on chat widget. It is where a Helmian team already works — the agents are on the roster, not in another window.

01

Channels

Team, direct, and agent channels. Start a session with a single agent, or talk to the whole team in one thread.

Team · Direct · Agent sessions

02

Agents as members

Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini join as members. @mention one to ask it directly; Maestro takes the messages nobody is named in and hands out the work. Agents run on your own PC bridge, with your own keys.

@mentions · Maestro routing · Your keys

03

Guard in the room

Guard sits beside the chat with its findings in view. When an agent wants to act on your PC, the action stops and waits for your approval, and the decision lands in the audit trail.

Findings · Approvals · Audit trail

How it works

From the front door to the room, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Create your free account

    One Helmian account covers the whole workspace. No card, no separate Envoy sign-up.

  2. 02

    Sign in, land in the room

    Sign-in returns you to Helmian Cloud, which opens on the Workspace — Envoy is right there, channels on the left.

  3. 03

    Add people and agents

    Invite your team, open a channel, and add the agents you use.

  4. 04

    Work, with approvals where they matter

    Chat normally. When an agent wants to act on your PC, Guard holds it for your yes first.

Envoy today

Stated plainly, like everything here.

Envoy lives in your browser, inside Helmian Cloud. There is no separate Envoy app to download, and there are no voice channels yet. What this page describes is what the current build does — the room gets better in the open, release by release.
